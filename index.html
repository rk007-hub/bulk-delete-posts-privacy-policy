Privacy Policy

Bulk Delete Posts for Facebook™ — last updated 30 August 2026

Short version: everything runs in your browser. We never see your Facebook data. There are no analytics and no tracking.

What the extension accesses

Facebook page content, in your own browser, only while you actively run the tool on facebook.com. It reads your posts' text and dates so it can filter and delete them at your instruction. It does not read other people's content.

What is stored (locally, on your device)

  • Your settings (deletion speed, advanced delay).
  • Progress of the current run, so it can resume after a reload or crash.
  • A lifetime counter of how many posts you've deleted on the free tier, and a random install identifier.
  • Your license status, once payments are available.

All of this lives in your browser's extension storage. It is never sent to us.

What is transmitted

  • To us: nothing. There is no server.
  • To the payment provider (once payments are live): only what's needed to check whether you've purchased. No Facebook data is ever involved.

No analytics, no tracking

There is no analytics SDK, no telemetry, no third-party scripts.
